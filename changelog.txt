RSC LINEUPS & TRYOUTS - CHANGE LOG
What changed, in plain language. Newest first.

======================================================================
v2.0  WEB APP  (11/25/2026)
======================================================================
The Google Sheet template was turned into a web page hosted on GitHub
Pages. It does the same jobs (Lineups and Tryouts) without anyone
making a copy of a sheet.

1. HOW IT WORKS
- Item: Where it lives
  Before: Each GM made their own copy of the Google Sheet template
  Now:    One web page for everyone. Fixes go live for all users at once

- Item: Player and tier data
  Before: Each copy pulled data with IMPORTRANGE and needed
          "Allow access" clicked on the Data tab
  Now:    A Google Apps Script reads the Contracts and Tier Maker sheets
          and serves the data to the page. Nothing to link or allow.
          Data is at most about 60 seconds old; Refresh re-reads it

- Item: Schedules
  Before: Hidden Schedules tab
  Now:    Built into the page. Same game orders as the sheet

- Item: Saving your work
  Before: Saved in your sheet copy
  Now:    Saved in your browser automatically. "Copy link" makes a link
          that opens your exact setup (players, tier, played games, wins)
          so you can share it in Discord

2. TIER MAKER PROTECTION
- Item: Tier Maker source (management only)
  Before: Anyone with the Tier Maker link pasted into their copy
  Now:    Tryouts > Player Source > Tier Maker asks for a passcode.
          The passcode is checked on the server; it is never in the page
          or in shared links. 20 wrong tries in 10 minutes locks it for
          10 minutes. "Lock" forgets the passcode on that browser

3. NEW IN THE WEB APP
- Tryouts: click "Blue won" or "Orange won" for each game (click again
  to undo) instead of typing a 1. Each player shows a W-L record
- Tryouts: wins are kept per schedule, so removing and re-adding the
  7th player does not lose results
- Lineups: changing the player in a slot clears only that slot's
  played lineups
- Lineups: names are matched without caring about capitals
  (e.g. "golf" becomes "Golf")
- "Copy for Discord" on Lineups (every lineup with total, room and a
  check or cross) and Tryouts (each result plus a W-L record)
- Status pill in the header: player count and data time. Click it to
  see each tier's min, max, cap and promoted MMR, plus any data notes
  (e.g. a tier with no promoted MMR uses the tier min)
- Works on phones and follows the device's dark/light mode
- Auto-refreshes the data every 5 minutes while the page is in use

   Fixes made after launch day testing:
   - Player list: names now show in the dropdown (the browser's built-in
     list hid them). Type to filter, click or press Enter to pick, arrow
     keys to move, Esc to close. Players already chosen are not offered
     again, and picking one jumps to the next empty slot
   - The title is now a link that starts over: it clears the saved setup
     and opens the clean page (the Tier Maker passcode is kept)
   - Player list shows each player's name and tier (Contracts column J).
     Tier Maker has no tier column, so its list shows names only
   - Base MMR (true MMR) and Eff. (Effective MMR, only for players
     promoted into the selected tier) are two columns beside Played
     (Lineups) or W-L (Tryouts) in the Players box. Show MMR off hides both
   - Light and dark themes redone for contrast and comfort, with a
     "Theme" button (Auto, Light, Dark) that is remembered. Native
     dropdowns, checkboxes and scrollbars follow the theme too
   - Footer: "Found a bug? DM w.ntr on Discord"
   - A "Totals" row in the Contracts Tiers tab was read as a tier. Summary
     rows with no min, max or cap are now ignored

4. SAME RULES AS THE SHEET
- Player list: Rostered, Free Agent, Permanent Free Agent,
  Perm FA in Waiting. and GM (Unsigned), sorted A to Z
- MMR: base MMR. A player from a lower tier counts at the selected
  tier's promoted MMR (highest Effective MMR in the second-to-last
  Keeper round), shaded amber. Falls back to the tier min if missing
- Room for 4th = cap minus the lineup total. Green: a tier max 4th
  fits. Red: won't fit even with a tier min 4th
- Show MMR off hides every MMR value and total
- A name not in the list is accepted; its MMR and totals stay blank

5. HOW IT WAS CHECKED
- Apps Script tested with fake Google services: status filter, tier
  limits, promoted MMR rule, caching, passcode accepted, rejected and
  locked out
- Every schedule compared with the sheet's Schedules tab: 5/6/7-player
  lineups (10/20/35, all unique), 6-player tryouts (10 splits),
  7 balanced (everyone sits once, every pair teams up exactly twice),
  14 (same with sides swapped), 70 (matches the sheet's order)
- Page tested in a browser with mock data: MMR and promoted players,
  totals and room colors, Played counts, W-L, Show MMR, shared links,
  Tier Maker lock, phone width and dark mode

======================================================================
v1.0  GOOGLE SHEET TEMPLATE  (Revision 13, 11/24/2026)
======================================================================
What changed from the original template, copied from the sheet's
Change Log tab.

1. TABS
- Lineups
  Before: 3 separate tabs: one each for 5, 6 and 7 players
  Now:    1 Lineups tab. Pick 5, 6 or 7 players
- Tryouts
  Before: 5 tabs: 6 and 7 players, each for Contracts and Tier Maker,
          plus a "Simple" 7-player tab
  Now:    1 Tryouts tab. A Player Source dropdown switches between
          Contracts and Tier Maker
- Reference tabs
  Before: 5 copies of player and tier data (GM Tier List,
          GM Tier List-MMR, 3s-Count/Keeper, 3s-Contracts, 3s-MMR)
  Now:    Removed. Data now comes straight from the two linked sheets
- Data
  Before: Spread across the reference tabs
  Now:    1 Data tab that pulls players, MMR and tiers from the links.
          Keep it visible: it is where users click "Allow access"
- Schedules
  Before: Game rotations built into each tab
  Now:    1 hidden Schedules tab shared by Lineups and Tryouts
- Instructions
  Before: 4 link rows (two links each for Contracts and Tier Maker)
  Now:    2 links: Contracts (required) and Tier Maker (optional,
          management only)
- Change Log
  Before: None
  Now:    A plain-language record of what changed

2. BUGS FIXED
- Tier Maker limits
  Before: On the Tier Maker tabs, the cap and tier limits depended on
          the "show MMR" link, so leaving that link blank broke them
  Now:    Cap and tier limits always work. The Show MMR checkbox only
          controls whether MMR is displayed
- 7-player layout
  Before: On the 7-player lineup tabs the top and bottom numbers were
          the opposite of the 6-player tabs
  Now:    One layout everywhere: lineup total on top, cap remainder below
- Player dropdown
  Before: Unsorted, and included people who are not active (Inactive
          Reserve, AGM IR, Former, Dropped)
  Now:    Sorted A to Z. Shows only Rostered, Free Agent, Permanent
          Free Agent, Perm FA in Waiting and GM (Unsigned)
- Leftover cells
  Before: Stray cells at the bottom of the 6-player Contracts tab
  Now:    Not carried over
- Blank-cell errors
  Before: Leaving the tier or cap blank could show error values
  Now:    Cap, tier limits, MMR and totals stay blank until a tier and
          players are chosen. With Show MMR off, or a typed name that
          has no MMR, totals stay blank instead of showing 0 or a
          partial total
- Number-only player names
  Before: A player whose name is a number (e.g. "22") showed a blank
          MMR, which blanked every lineup total containing them
  Now:    Number names look up correctly on both tabs, including the
          amber promoted-MMR shading
- Lineups player dropdown
  Before: Picking a number-only name (e.g. "22") from the Lineups
          dropdown was rejected as invalid data
  Now:    The dropdown accepts it, like the Tryouts dropdown. A name
          not in the list is also accepted, but its MMR and totals
          stay blank
- Lineups edit warnings
  Before: The Lineups tab warned on every edit, including the player
          and tier dropdowns, Show MMR and Played boxes
  Now:    Those input cells no longer warn. Formulas and layout still do

3. NEW FEATURES
- Show MMR
  Before: Hide MMR by leaving a link row blank
  Now:    A checkbox on each tab (Lineups and Tryouts)
- Effective MMR
  Before: Showed each player's MMR for their own tier
  Now:    Shows base MMR. A player promoted from a lower tier gets the
          highest MMR in the selected tier's second-to-last Keeper
          round (round 5 when there are 6), taken from the Count/Keeper
          tab (Contracts) or GM Tier List (Tier Maker). Shaded amber.
          Players already in the tier keep their own MMR
- Balanced 7-player schedule
  Before: Only the full list of every possible split
  Now:    7 games (everyone sits once, teams up with each other player
          twice), 14 games (the same with sides swapped), or all 70
          splits
- Accidental-edit protection
  Before: Nothing protected, so formulas could be overwritten by mistake
  Now:    A warning appears if someone edits formulas, schedules, data
          or helper columns. Input cells (players, tier, settings, wins,
          Played, links) stay open. It is a warning, not a lock
- Lineups Played tracker
  Before: A 7-column Count table showing each player's running total
          at every game, which was hard to read
  Now:    Tick a game's Played checkbox after playing it. The game greys
          out, and a single Played column beside the players shows how
          many games each has played. Players behind the others are
          shaded amber

4. LOOK AND CLEANUP
- Design
  Before: Plain tables
  Now:    Title banners, blue and orange teams, tinted and gridded empty
          game blocks, cream input cells, frozen headers
- Cleanup
  Before: Extra empty columns and rows, duplicate data copies
  Now:    Removed. Helper columns are hidden
- Ready to ship
  Before: Links and test entries left in
  Now:    Links and test entries blank. Last updated 11/24/2026

5. HOW IT WAS CHECKED
Lineups (5, 6 and 7 players) and Tryouts (6 and 7 players, both
sources) were compared against the original tabs. The 7-game schedule
was verified by script. Show MMR, blank links, typed names and
effective MMR were tested with real players. The Played tracker was
tested with 7 players: ticked games counted correctly and a player with
0 games was flagged. Promoted MMR was tested on both tabs with both
sources, including a number-only name ("22") and the Tier Maker source.
Players already in the tier (e.g. Veteran at 1360 and 1370) kept their
own MMR.
